Independent emissions estimates from satellites: Climate TRACE

What this video covers
How investors and local governments check a company's reported greenhouse gas emissions against an independent, satellite-based estimate. Climate TRACE publishes free facility-level estimates for about 2.76 million sources. Where it fits, what it decides, the limits in 2026, and how to think about cost. About 6 minutes.
