Watching grain-growing regions with free government satellite maps (JASMAI)

What this video covers
How a Japanese government system turns JAXA and NASA satellite data into free maps of weather and vegetation over the world's grain-growing regions. Who uses it, where it fits in procurement, what it does not show, and the cost. 6 minutes.
