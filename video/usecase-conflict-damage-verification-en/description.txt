Verifying conflict damage with satellite data and AI

What this video covers
How a broadcaster used satellite data and AI to verify conflict damage in Myanmar, where reporters could not go. Where it fits in the reporting workflow, what decisions it supports, how far free satellites go, and why imagery alone cannot tell you the cause. About 6 minutes.
