Checking EUDR sourcing plots for deforestation with satellite data

What this video covers
How satellite-based deforestation maps help companies meet the EU Deforestation Regulation, which applies from 30 December 2026. Where it fits in due diligence, what it decides, a published software price, and why shade-grown farms and missing coordinates keep field checks in the loop. About 6 minutes.
