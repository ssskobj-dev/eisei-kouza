Mapping coral reef habitats with free satellite data

What this video covers
How governments and NGOs narrow marine protected area work using the free, satellite-built Allen Coral Atlas. Where it fits in the workflow, what decisions it supports, what the free maps cover, where the cost falls, and the limits as of 2026. About 6 minutes.
