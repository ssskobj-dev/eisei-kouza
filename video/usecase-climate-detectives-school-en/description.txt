Satellite data in the classroom: ESA Climate Detectives for teens

What this video covers
How schools let students study a local environmental problem with free satellite data, using Europe's Climate Detectives project as the model. Where it fits in a class, what it can and cannot show, how far free satellites go, and what it costs. About 6 minutes.
