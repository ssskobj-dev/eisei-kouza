Satellite data for mineral exploration: ASTER alteration mapping

What this video covers
How resource companies narrow metal-exploration targets by mapping hydrothermal alteration zones from free ASTER multispectral data, as reported by Japan's national resource agency JOGMEC in Chile. Where it fits, what it decides, how far free data goes, and the limits as of 2026. About 6 minutes.
