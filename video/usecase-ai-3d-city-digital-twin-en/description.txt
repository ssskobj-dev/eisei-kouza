Building 3D cities from satellites to show flood risk to residents

What this video covers
How satellite imagery plus AI builds photoreal 3D cities so flood and sea-level-rise scenarios can be shown to residents, used in Tonga and Ghana with UN and Commonwealth partners. Where it fits, what it decides, and why it is not a substitute for survey. About 6 minutes.
