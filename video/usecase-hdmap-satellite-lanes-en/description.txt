Mapping lanes for self-driving cars from space | Satellite Data

What this video covers
How Toyota's TRI-AD, Maxar and NTT DATA generated high-definition road maps from commercial satellite imagery, reaching about 25 cm relative accuracy in a 2020 proof of concept. What the result really means, what satellites cannot see, and why the cost saving was only "expected". 6 minutes.
