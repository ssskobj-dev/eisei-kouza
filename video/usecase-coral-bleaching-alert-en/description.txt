Satellite data for daily coral bleaching alerts, free from NOAA

What this video covers
NOAA's Coral Reef Watch turns satellite sea-surface temperature into a daily heat-stress alert for 95% of the world's coral reefs, on a 5 km grid, for free. We cover how the alert is built, where it fits in a reef manager's workflow, what decisions it supports, its limits as of 2026, and why the data is free but dive surveys remain. 6 minutes.
