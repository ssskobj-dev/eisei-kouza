Satellite data to verify methane in the LNG supply chain

What this video covers
How LNG buyers and banks can cross-check a supplier's methane emissions with satellites. A Japanese government project is testing a "Tip and Cue" method - a wide-area government satellite flags suspect areas, then a high-resolution commercial constellation looks closely. We cover where it fits, what you can decide, the 2026 limits, and where the cost sits. 6 minutes.
