Tracking light pollution at dark-sky sites with satellites

What this video covers
How towns that sell their night sky watch the surrounding glow with free satellite night-light data. A 2021 study found upward light fell about 1.6% a year in and around 98 certified dark-sky places. Where it fits, what it decides, how far free data goes, and its limits as of 2026. About 6 minutes.
