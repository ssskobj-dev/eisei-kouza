What this video covers
A plain map of the players in satellite forest and carbon monitoring - who provides the data, who turns it into carbon numbers, who sets the rules and grades the quality, and who pays. Company names are given only as documented examples, not recommendations. Figures are from Verra, NASA, ESA, Planet and WRI, with the source stated for each. About 6 minutes.
