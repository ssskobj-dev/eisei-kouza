Reading the Arctic shipping route with satellite sea ice

What this video covers
How a shipping line decides whether to route through the Arctic, using a satellite sea-ice forecast of when the route opens. In 2019 the summer forecast said "mid-August"; the route actually opened on August 20. Where it fits in the workflow, what it decides, the width of the forecast, and how far free data goes. 6 minutes.
