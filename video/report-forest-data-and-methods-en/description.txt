Forests and carbon from orbit: the data and the methods | Satellite Data

What this video covers
Which satellites and which data actually sit behind forest and carbon monitoring - optical for where trees are lost, radar to see through tropical cloud, and lidar to measure what the carbon weighs. Much of it is free and open. The sensors, where paid data begins, how the analysis runs, and where the real cost sits. About 6 minutes.
