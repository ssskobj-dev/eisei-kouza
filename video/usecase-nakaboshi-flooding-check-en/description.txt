Checking rice-paddy carbon credits with satellite SAR

What this video covers
How Japanese demonstrations use satellite SAR to cross-check rice-paddy carbon credits from extending mid-season drainage. Where it fits as a screening step, what it can and cannot decide, how far free satellites go, and a cost estimate for paid imagery. Records and photos stay the official evidence. About 6 minutes.
