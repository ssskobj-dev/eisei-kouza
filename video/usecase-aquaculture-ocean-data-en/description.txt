Daily satellite ocean data maps for aquaculture feeding decisions

What this video covers
How fish, shellfish and seaweed farmers use daily satellite and ocean-model maps of water temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, chlorophyll and wave height to decide feeding and harvest timing. Where it fits, what it decides, its limits, and how the free base data and paid service split. 6 minutes.
