What this video covers
Three documented overseas cases of satellite monitoring for forests and carbon - a free global forest-loss service, the EU regulation now driving demand, and a methane-detection satellite in operation. What they measure, how to read the numbers, and what a consultant should take from them. About 6 minutes.
