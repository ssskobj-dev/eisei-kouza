Free satellite imagery for big disasters (International Charter)

What this video covers
How national disaster authorities get satellite imagery for free when a major disaster hits, through the International Charter. Who can request it, where it fits in the response, what decisions it supports, its 2026 limits, and the cost picture for the side that receives it. About 6 minutes.
